# Interprofessional Education (IPE) Simulation Student Handout
**DENTHYG 3880 Interprofessional Education Simulation**
**Comprehensive Care for an Older Adult with Complex Health Needs**

## Purpose
Healthcare professionals work together to address the complex needs of patients whose health concerns extend beyond a single discipline. This simulation provides an opportunity to collaborate with learners from multiple professions to develop a patient-centered care plan.
You will work as members of an interprofessional healthcare team caring for a patient with chronic health conditions, oral health concerns, mobility limitations, and social challenges.

## Learning Objectives
Upon completion of this activity, learners will be able to:

### Interprofessional Collaboration
- Describe the roles and responsibilities of dental hygiene, dentistry, medicine, nursing, occupational therapy, physical therapy, and social work in patient care.
- Demonstrate effective interprofessional communication skills when discussing patient assessment findings and treatment recommendations.
- Develop a collaborative, patient-centered care plan that incorporates the expertise of multiple healthcare professionals.

### Patient-Centered Care
- Identify the patient's immediate health concerns and prioritize interventions based on patient needs and goals.
- Analyze how oral health, systemic health, functional status, mental health, and social determinants of health influence one another.

### Clinical Decision-Making
- Recognize risk factors contributing to poor health outcomes, including chronic disease, falls, medication adherence challenges, and limited access to healthcare.
- Recommend evidence-based interventions and appropriate referrals to improve health outcomes and quality of life.

## Patient Case

### Patient Profile
- **Name:** Linda Johnson
- **Age:** 67 years
- **Occupation:** Retired Elementary School Teacher
- **Living Situation:** Lives alone in a second-floor apartment without elevator access
- **Insurance:** Medicare with limited supplemental coverage

### Chief Concern
"I've been having trouble getting around since I fell a few months ago. I'm tired all the time, my mouth hurts when I eat, and I don't feel like myself anymore."

### Medical History
- Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
- Hypertension
- Osteoarthritis
- Mild Depression
- Obesity (BMI 33)
- Peripheral Neuropathy
- Recent Fall (3 months ago)

### Current Medications
- Metformin
- Lisinopril
- Atorvastatin
- Gabapentin
- Sertraline

### Social History
- Widowed
- Daughter lives two hours away
- Limited income
- Transportation difficulties
- Social isolation
- Occasionally skips medications due to cost
- Has not seen a dentist in five years

### Current Symptoms
The patient reports:
- Pain when chewing
- Bleeding gums
- Dry mouth
- Poor appetite
- Unintentional weight loss (10 pounds)
- Fear of falling
- Knee pain
- Fatigue
- Difficulty managing diabetes
- Feelings of loneliness and sadness

## Clinical Findings

### Vital Signs
- **Blood Pressure:** 152/92 mmHg
- **Pulse:** 88 bpm
- **HbA1c:** 9.2%

### Oral Health Findings
- Generalized moderate-to-severe periodontal disease
- Gingival inflammation and bleeding
- Multiple carious lesions
- Xerostomia
- Heavy plaque accumulation
- Ill-fitting partial denture
- Several mobile teeth

## Team Activity Instructions

### Step 1: Individual Assessment (10 minutes)
Review the patient case from the perspective of your profession.
Discuss:
- What are your primary concerns?
- What additional information would you like to gather?
- What interventions would you recommend?

### Step 2: Interprofessional Team Discussion (20 minutes)
Share your profession's assessment with the team.
Together, determine:
- What are the patient's most urgent needs?
- What factors are contributing to the patient's current health status?
- How do oral health and systemic health interact in this case?
- Which professionals should take the lead on specific issues?
- What referrals are needed?

### Step 3: Care Plan Development (15 minutes)
As a team, develop a coordinated care plan that includes:
- Short-Term Goals (1 Month)
- Long-Term Goals (3–6 Months)
- Referrals Needed

## Profession-Specific Reflection Questions

### Dental Hygiene & Dental Students
- How might periodontal disease affect diabetes management?
- What oral health concerns require immediate attention?
- How could oral pain impact nutrition and overall health?

### Occupational Therapy Students
- What challenges might the patient face with daily activities?
- What adaptations could improve independence and safety?
- How might medication management be improved?

### Physical Therapy Students
- What fall-risk factors are present?
- What interventions would improve strength and balance?
- What mobility assessments would be beneficial?

### Medical & Nursing Students
- What concerns exist regarding diabetes control and hypertension?
- What barriers may be affecting medication adherence?
- What health education should be prioritized?

### Social Work Students
- What social determinants of health are affecting this patient?
- What community resources may be helpful?
- How can isolation and financial barriers be addressed?

## Discussion Questions
- Which issue should be addressed first and why?
- How are oral health problems contributing to the patient's overall health challenges?
- What barriers might prevent the patient from following through with treatment recommendations?
- How can the healthcare team support the patient's goals and preferences?
- What does successful interprofessional collaboration look like in this case?

## Debrief Reflection
After the activity, reflect on the following:
- What did you learn about another profession's role in patient care?
- How did collaboration improve the team's care plan?
- What challenges arose during team communication?
- How will this experience influence your future professional practice?

## Key Takeaway
Optimal patient outcomes depend on effective communication, mutual respect, and collaboration among healthcare professionals. Oral health, systemic health, mental health, mobility, and social factors are interconnected and require a coordinated team-based approach.
