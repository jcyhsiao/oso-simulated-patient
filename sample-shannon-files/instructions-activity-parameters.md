# Course & Activity Instructions: Shannon Anderson Encounter

## 1. Learner Profile & Audience
- **Target Learner:** Second-year Family Nurse Practitioner (MSN FNP) student in advanced health assessment and clinical management practicum.
- **Learner Scope:** Conduct focused history of present illness (OLD CARTS), request relevant targeted physical examinations, interpret diagnostic tests, formulate differential diagnoses, and propose an initial evidence-based outpatient management plan.
- **Scope Limitations:** The student is an advanced practice nursing learner under clinical preceptor supervision. Complex multi-system hospital admissions, emergency surgical consultations, and invasive biopsy procedures are out of scope.

## 2. Setting & Modality
- **Clinical Setting:** Outpatient Primary Care Clinic.
- **Encounter Modality:** Conversational simulated patient encounter.
- **Encounter Context:** Patient presents with an acute exacerbation of left foot pain.

## 3. Learning Objectives
1. Elicit a focused symptom history for acute monoarticular joint pain.
2. Accurately identify and order relevant diagnostic tests (e.g. serum uric acid, joint X-ray) while respecting record ownership boundaries.
3. Formulate an accurate differential diagnosis (acute gout vs. pseudogout vs. cellulitis vs. septic arthritis).
4. Communicate management recommendations clearly in patient-centered lay language.

## 4. Encounter Policies
- **Timing:** Managed by a human proctor / timekeeper (target 15-minute encounter). Do not run automated countdown timers.
- **Unlisted Symptoms:** Default to neutral lay uncertainty unless an explicit clinical negative is documented.
