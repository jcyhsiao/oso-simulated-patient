- Do not change `web/content/runtime.md` except where this document specifies.

- Saved cases (IndexedDB, `.spcase.zip`) from the current version must still open. Keep `schema_version: 1`; add new fields as optional with defaults.

### WS2 — Unlisted-symptom policy (per-case, default neutral)

**Model field:** `unlistedSymptoms: 'neutral' | 'deny'`, default `'neutral'`.

**UI** (`index.template.html`, in the Patient material card, `data-area-panel="patient-parameters-and-disclosable-info"`, below the input group): a `<fieldset>` with two radios `name="unlisted-symptoms"`:
- `neutral` (checked by default) — label: **"Stay neutral (recommended)"**, help: "If a learner asks about a symptom your materials don't mention, the patient neither invents it nor denies it."
- `deny` — label: **"Deny it"**, help: "The patient answers with a natural 'no'. Use only if your materials list every symptom the patient has."

**App** (`app.mjs`): include in `model()`; `change` → `update()`; restore in `caseSession.apply` (missing → `'neutral'`).

**Storage** (`case-storage.mjs::cleanCaseModel`): accept `'neutral'|'deny'`, default `'neutral'` when absent; reject other values with "Invalid case settings."

**Runtime** (`web/content/runtime.md`): replace current lines 19–20 (the two bullets under "NEVER make up or improvise…") with exactly one marker line:

```
[[UNLISTED_DETAIL_POLICY]]
```

**Compiler** (`compiler.mjs`): before assembling `directInstructions`, replace the marker. If the marker is missing from `content.runtime`, throw `Error('Runtime is missing the unlisted-detail policy marker.')`. Replacement text:

Neutral (default):
```
- Symptoms the materials neither list as present nor explicitly deny are UNSPECIFIED. Do not invent them and do not deny them as a fact. Reply with brief, non-committal lay uncertainty (e.g., "Hmm, that's not something I've really paid attention to"). State explicitly supplied negatives as written.
- Biographical, lifestyle, dietary or historical details not in the materials: reply briefly in character that you are not sure or have nothing particular to add. Unspecified information is never converted into a positive or negative finding.
```

Deny (faculty-selected):
```
- Faculty policy for this case: if the learner asks about a symptom or complaint the materials do not list as present, answer with a natural lay negative (e.g., "No, I haven't noticed anything like that"). This applies to symptoms only.
- Biographical, lifestyle, dietary or historical details not in the materials: reply briefly in character that you are not sure or have nothing particular to add. Apart from the symptom rule above, unspecified information is never converted into a positive or negative finding.
```

Also record `unlisted_symptoms` in the manifest.

Acceptance: default compile contains "UNSPECIFIED" and not "Faculty policy for this case"; `deny` compile is the reverse; the marker string never appears in output.

### WS3 — Rewrite the 11 professional profiles as general learner-role profiles

Files: all of `web/content/professionals/*.md` (dental-hygiene, dentistry, dermatology, ent, environmental-health, housing-advocacy, nursing, occupational-therapy, physical-therapy, primary-care, social-work).

Purpose shift: these describe **the learner's profession** for (a) the hosting model's understanding of who is interviewing the patient and (b) role-appropriate preliminary feedback. They must **not** describe a simulated consultant, a handoff recipient, a speaking style, or response-length rules.

**Exact template** (keep `# PROFESSIONAL:` prefix — `web/build.mjs` parses titles from it):

```
# PROFESSIONAL: <Title>

## Role and scope
<2–3 sentences: who this learner is in a patient encounter, the setting(s) they typically work in, and that credential level, licensure and local permissions (e.g., prescribing, ordering, procedures) are defined by the program and faculty criteria, not by this profile.>

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
<3–5 bullets: what this profession characteristically attends to when interviewing/assessing a patient.>

## Contributions within scope
<3–5 bullets: actions/judgments generally within this profession's scope (education, assessment components, planning, referral, coordination).>

## Scope considerations for feedback
<2–4 bullets: what is generally outside this profession's scope so feedback does not penalize omitting it; phrase as "Do not expect…" rather than prohibitions on the learner.>

## Interprofessional collaboration
<1–3 bullets: typical collaborators and when to involve them.>
```

Rules for the rewrite:
- 700–1,300 characters per profile (all 11 combined ≤ 14,000). Count with `wc -m`.
- Remove all of: `ID:/Version` lines, "Visible judgment process", "Focused information needs", "Communication example", "Reuse rules", "Review metadata", "Progressive response rule", any "consultation question", "handoff", "the learner's request", word-count limits.
- No absolute prohibitions that conflict with advanced-practice programs. Example: Primary Care must **not** say "do not prescribe"; instead "Prescribing and ordering authority depend on the learner's credential and program; faculty criteria define what is expected." The Primary Care title should cover advanced practice: `Primary care (including advanced practice: FNP/AGPCNP, PA, physician)`.
- Generic and evidence-cautious: no invented statistics, guidelines, or jurisdiction-specific claims.
- Keep the first-line note behavior in the UI ("AI-generated starter drafts for faculty review") unchanged.

## 5. Suggested live-trial probes for the user afterwards

1. Ask about a symptom the materials don't mention (expect neutral reply by default).
2. Order a lab/imaging study not in the materials (expect the "No scripted findings…" facilitator line).
3. Ask to see the affected area (expect the visual description only, no lab or imaging reports).
4. Open-ended "Tell me about the pain" (expect 1–2 sentences, not the full HPI table).
5. Switch the case to "Deny it", recompile, repeat probe 1.
