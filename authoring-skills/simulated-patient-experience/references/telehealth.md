# Level 1 telehealth patient experience

Base authoring guidance; case-independent and not a prescribed faculty submission format.

Establish the communication channel (text, voice or video), patient/learner roles, available clinical information, encounter boundaries and supported assets. Keep patient disclosure grounded in supplied knowledge. Natural greetings should not trigger an unsolicited chart dump.

Identify what can be obtained through conversation, patient demonstration or an available image. Route supplied recorded findings separately; do not imply that palpation, auscultation or a physical examination occurred through chat. Distinguish requested findings from rescue prompts.

Extract any required identity, consent, privacy, location or emergency procedures from the supplied course/institution directions. Flag missing requirements without inventing their completion or adding patient biography. Confirm asset access and approved fallback text in the actual delivery host.

Produce a capability/limitation statement and a role/release map. Leave timing, rubric, passing standards and feedback timing to the activity layer. Choosing telehealth does not select an OSCE or authorize scoring.
