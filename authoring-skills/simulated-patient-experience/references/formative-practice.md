# Level 3 formative practice activity

Project-designed authoring template. Use when the stated purpose is practice rather than assuming every simulation is an assessment.

Use [evidence-and-review.md](evidence-and-review.md) to ground review in simulation standards and distinguish indirect educational evidence from AI validation. Plan preparation/briefing and post-encounter feedback, guided reflection or debriefing; identify missing plans before educational deployment. The current Shannon runtime supplies an encounter, not a complete automated debriefing course.

Identify the target skill, learner task, completion conditions, permitted resources and available feedback. Propose reflection, evidence-grounded debrief and another attempt where useful; distinguish proposals from supplied requirements.

Do not make the patient the tutor. Keep instructional feedback in a distinct facilitator/debrief role and at an authorized boundary. Any during-encounter rescue still needs faculty-defined triggers. Track assistance when interpreting performance.

Ask the learner to explain reasoning through a separate submission only when the activity requires it; do not infer complete clinical reasoning from an interview transcript. Repetition of the same case can support practice but does not by itself demonstrate transfer to another case. Do not invent grades, mastery thresholds or validated rubric claims.
