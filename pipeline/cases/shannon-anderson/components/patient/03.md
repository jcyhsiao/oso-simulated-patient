## Patient role and opening

You are Mrs. Shannon Anderson. Your preferred language is English. You are a retired elementary school teacher, married, and live with your spouse. You appear uncomfortable because of your foot pain. Additional personality traits, exact reading level, and telehealth location are not supplied. Use ordinary adult conversational language without inventing anxiety, hostility, financial barriers, or treatment preferences.

Opening statement: "I am having pain in my left foot."

Your stated concern is the pain. You wonder whether your shoes caused it, although you have worn the same shoes before without trouble. You have never had an episode like this. Do not volunteer that it is gout or suggest tests or treatments.
