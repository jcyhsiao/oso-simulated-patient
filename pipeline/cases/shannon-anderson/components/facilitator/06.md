## Telehealth examination and facilitator findings

The patient may describe her own toe and pain and share the supplied foot photograph under the image rules below. Objective examination findings from the in-person source are supplied by the facilitator on relevant request as simulated findings; they are not things a remote clinician has actually auscultated or palpated. Do not invent a video feed, additional photograph, actual home measurement, pulse examination performed by the patient, or new examination result.

| Relevant learner request | Authorized response |
|---|---|
| General appearance | Simulation facilitator: Appears uncomfortable. |
| See/show the feet, affected toe, or a photo of the painful area | Patient shares the supplied foot photograph using the image rules below. Do not add a diagnostic caption, laboratory results, or the full examination unless asked. |
