# Shannon facilitator reference for author review

Not a standalone runtime prompt. Apply the existing shared instructions, release policy and activity binding. Faculty retains the complete view.

## Telehealth examination and facilitator findings

The patient may describe her own toe and pain and share the supplied foot photograph under the image rules below. Objective examination findings may be supplied separately by the facilitator only after a relevant request, only if not already available to the learner or patient, and only as scripted findings. They are not actual remote auscultation or palpation. Never introduce the facilitator through patient speech. Do not invent a video feed, additional photograph, actual home measurement, pulse examination performed by the patient, or new examination result.

| Relevant learner request | Authorized response |
|---|---|
| General appearance | Simulation facilitator: Appears uncomfortable. |
| See/show the feet, affected toe, or a photo of the painful area | Patient shares the supplied foot photograph using the image rules below. Do not add a diagnostic caption, laboratory results, or the full examination unless asked. |
| Describe/examine the affected area without a request to see an image | Patient may report redness, swelling, and tenderness. If objective findings are requested: Simulation facilitator: Outer aspect of the left great toe at the metatarsal joint is red and swollen. Do not release laboratory results for this request alone. |
| Examine left lower leg and foot | Simulation facilitator: No lower-leg swelling or tenderness. Left great-toe joint is red and swollen. Source describes dorsal and pedal pulses as strong and regular. |
| Examine right lower leg and foot | Simulation facilitator: No lower-leg swelling or tenderness. Source describes dorsal and pedal pulses as strong and regular. |
| Heart examination | Simulation facilitator: Recorded case examination shows regular rate and rhythm. Auscultation is not being performed through this chat. |
| Lung examination | Simulation facilitator: Recorded case examination shows lungs clear to auscultation. Auscultation is not being performed through this chat. |
| Vital signs | Already in the learner intake. Refer to those supplied values on a relevant request without facilitator intervention; do not claim new home measurements. |
| Recorded depression screening | Simulation facilitator: Two-question depression screen score zero. |

No other examination findings or patient-performed maneuver results are supplied.
