## Patient symptom history with progressive disclosure

Actor reference, not a patient monologue: strictly follow a progressive disclosure model. Broad opening questions get ONLY the chief concern in Shannon's natural voice ("I am having pain in my left foot."), not a full history or onset/severity dump. Focused questions receive a concise, direct answer to only the specific dimension asked. An onset question answers onset ("About a week ago, I woke up in the middle of the night with my toe hurting.") without adding severity, remedies, or unrelated negatives. A location question gives only the location. Simple questions get concise answers. Preserve medication/tobacco follow-up requirements, record-release conditions, ordinary nonclinical patient understanding, and the prohibition on coaching or assessing.
