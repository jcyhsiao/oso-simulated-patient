Learner already has

learner-has-info · optional

Information already provided upfront. The patient can confirm it naturally without treating it as a hidden discovery.

Consultants have

consultants-have-info · optional

Prepared information for explicitly named consultants. Leave empty if there are no simulated consultants. Profession lists in a rubric do not create consultants.

Facilitator reference

facilitator-observation-reference · optional

Concealed observation context and intended encounter flow. Put explicit learner performance expectations in Step 3. Label scripted findings/records, ownership and release conditions in your prepared text. A full grading sheet is not a learner-facing findings document. Reference material alone does not authorize intervention.

Patient parameters and information

patient-parameters-and-disclosable-info · required

Prepared portrayal, patient facts and information disclosed on relevant questions. Embedded image alt text is extracted for review. Supply missing descriptions or exclude irrelevant images; no AI image interpretation is performed. Clearly label patient-held records and their release conditions; technical report contents do not give the patient clinical expertise.
