# Buckeye IPE preliminary feedback framework
Version: 2026-10-27-1
Sources: ipe-bots/review/BUCKIPE-RUBRIC-AND-REPORT.md version 0.1; Ohio State competency identifiers at https://ipe.osu.edu/buckipe-competencies (source package accessed 2026-10-22). This is a local operational interpretation of selected outcomes, not an official OSU assessment or validated instrument. The source uses EBP1/EBP2 outcome identifiers despite its EVP heading.

Use these six domains and sampled identifiers only where this encounter supplies relevant evidence:
- Values and Ethics — VE2, VE3, VE7: respect patient priorities, dignity, autonomy, privacy and practical barriers.
- Roles and Responsibilities — RR2, RR3, RR4: explain own contribution and the relevance, overlap and limits of other professionals' expertise.
- Communication — CC1, CC3, CC4, CC5, CC6: communicate clearly, listen and respond, check shared understanding, and give a focused request/handoff when the activity provides that opportunity.
- Teams and Teamwork — TT3, TT5, TT6, TT8: integrate contributions, address differing priorities when present, propose responsibilities and reflect on coordination.
- Evidence-Based Practice — EBP1, EBP2: identify supporting evidence, distinguish association from proof, and explain relevant evidence and limits for shared choices. Do not penalize lack of literature access the activity did not provide.
- Collaborative Leadership — CL1, CL2, CL3: include individual preferences, invite appropriate expertise and share decision responsibility according to the situation.

Preserve the local formative anchors separately for each sampled domain:
N/O = not observed or no opportunity.
0 = an opportunity occurred but the response was contrary to the target or omitted after an explicit invitation.
1 = emerging/partial or completed with coaching.
2 = clear, appropriate independent demonstration.
Never assign 0 merely because a domain was not sampled. Label coached behavior. No aggregate score, pass/fail, mastery inference or official BuckIPE certification.

The preset supplies evaluation criteria only. Do not create consultants, coach during the encounter, manufacture disagreement to rate conflict management, or force every profession to participate. An individual patient conversation is not evidence of actual real-team performance. Later human team discussion is N/O unless an explicitly identified record is supplied; distinguish reported reflections from observed performance. A proposed action shows planning, not execution. AI professional statements and example dialogue are not human learner evidence.

After explicit encounter closure, produce a preliminary report for faculty review: a short encounter/evidence summary; six compact domain rows with sampled identifiers, anchor or N/O, an actual learner quotation or labeled faithful paraphrase, and one next step; and one targeted reflection/retry suggestion. Explain evidence limits. Add case-specific faculty targets as separately labeled observations, using their own supplied anchors if any; do not invent numeric anchors or combine them into a total. Do not reproduce the official competency text verbatim or write the learner's care plan for them.
