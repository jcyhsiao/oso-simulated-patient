## Online simulation and human timekeeping

Preserve the fictional clinical setting supplied by faculty; online delivery does not itself turn an office case into a clinical telehealth visit. The chatbot provides an online simulation, not a real examination, diagnostic service, recording system or timer.

A human assessor/timekeeper manages assessment duration, warnings and the stop signal. The chatbot must not invent elapsed time, announce its own time warning or terminate the encounter by counting turns. The human can send END ENCOUNTER, STOP or another explicit live end instruction. An intermediate timing warning does not itself close the visit. Historical room, recording and overhead-warning instructions in uploaded documents do not establish actual platform capabilities.

Distinguish learner-stated simulated examination actions and requested scripted findings from actually observed physical performance. Faculty determine what evidence each criterion requires. Do not infer either a completed real examination or an automatic failure from online delivery alone.

## Prepare source content

Resolve clinical contradictions or supply clearly labeled faculty clarifications tied to the affected statements. File order alone does not resolve conflicts. Label records/findings with their ownership, dates, availability and release conditions. Keep concealed grading/treatment commentary separate from releasable evidence, and place concise case-specific feedback targets in Step 3. The builder cannot determine which free-text clinical statement is correct or whether a clinical target is valid.

## Fresh-chat checks in the destination host

- Greet and ask a combined question: verify natural, scoped patient entry without a history dump.
- Confirm upfront facts: verify no artificial concealment, recall variation or facilitator handoff.
- Ask about previous evaluation: verify discovery of an explicitly authored existing record without a full results dump.
- Request a result, give a firm plan/order, or obtain consent to a matching supplied test: verify same-response delivery under faculty release conditions, with original dates and no extra result request.
- Discuss a hypothetical, cancelled, wrong-site or unsupplied test: verify no invented or incorrectly unlocked results.
- Request a scripted examination finding: verify a separate simulated finding without claiming a real physical examination was performed.
- Request a visual description: verify only reviewed details under their release conditions, without requiring image generation/display.
- Give a live time warning and then an explicit human end signal: verify no imaginary countdown, premature closure or unsupported timing grade.
- End explicitly: verify separate, selected preliminary feedback based on actual learner evidence and its limits. A plan or invitation for patient questions alone should not end the encounter.

Record actual host/model, package version, inputs and fresh-chat outcomes. These checks are proposed host trials, not proof that a model will follow every instruction or that an assessment is validated.
