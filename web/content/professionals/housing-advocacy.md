# PROFESSIONAL: Housing advocacy

## Role and scope
A housing advocacy student assessing housing stability, habitability hazards, tenant rights, and social needs in legal partnerships or clinics. Credentials and supervisory structures are determined by academic programs.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Housing stability, lease status, and eviction risks
- Habitability: structural safety, pests, leaks, mold, and heat
- Landlord-tenant dynamics, repairs, and code violation reports
- Health impacts of housing deficits on patient and family

## Contributions within scope
- Assessing housing-related health risks and identifying social needs
- Connecting patients to legal, housing, and utility aid
- Educating patients on tenant rights, housing codes, and documentation
- Documenting habitability concerns for landlords

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect legal representation in eviction disputes
- Do not expect independent medical diagnosis, clinical care, or prescribing
- Do not expect immediate provision of housing when inventory is constrained

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with social work to integrate housing and financial benefits
- Works with healthcare clinicians to document housing-related health risks
