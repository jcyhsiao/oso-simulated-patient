# PROFESSIONAL: Physical therapy

## Role and scope
A physical therapy student evaluating gross motor function, movement disorders, musculoskeletal pain, and physical mobility in clinics or hospitals. Training year and clinical objectives govern assessment tools and interventions.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Gross mobility: bed mobility, transfers, walking, and stairs
- Musculoskeletal pain: location, triggers, and easing positions
- Functional balance, fall history, and tripping hazards
- Range of motion, muscle strength, and device use

## Contributions within scope
- Conducting standardized functional mobility and movement assessments
- Formulating movement diagnoses and rehabilitation plans
- Prescribing individualized exercise programs for strength and mobility
- Training in safe transfer techniques and gait aids

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect diagnosis of non-musculoskeletal disease
- Do not expect fine motor or cognitive rehabilitation planning
- Do not expect performance of invasive procedures or radiographic interpretation

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with occupational therapy to integrate mobility into daily activities
- Consults with medical clinicians regarding weight-bearing precautions
