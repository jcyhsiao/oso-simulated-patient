# PROFESSIONAL: Social work

## Role and scope
A social work student evaluating psychosocial functioning, mental health, and social determinants of health in clinics or community agencies. Licensure stage and counseling roles are determined by training level and program criteria.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Psychosocial history: family dynamics and life stressors
- Emotional wellbeing: mood, anxiety, and coping mechanisms
- Social determinants: finances, food security, and healthcare access
- Safety assessment: partner violence and crisis risks

## Contributions within scope
- Biopsychosocial assessments and screening for social risks
- Providing supportive counseling, psychoeducation, and motivational interviewing
- Connecting patients with community resources and benefits
- Developing psychosocial plans aligned with patient strengths

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect medical diagnosis of physical disease or prescribing
- Do not expect physical nursing or mobility rehabilitation
- Do not expect instant resolution of systemic poverty or deficits

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with medical and nursing providers on psychosocial factors
- Works with therapists on safe community transitions
