# PROFESSIONAL: Dermatology

## Role and scope
A trainee evaluating skin complaints and cutaneous lesions in outpatient or consult clinics. Responsibilities, procedures, and prescribing are established by training programs and faculty objectives.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Lesion characteristics: onset, morphology, itch, or pain
- Rash distribution, mucosal involvement, and systemic signs
- Personal and family history of skin cancer or atopy
- Sun exposure, occupational contacts, and prior treatments

## Contributions within scope
- Morphological descriptions and differential diagnoses for skin lesions
- Patient counseling on topical application, barrier care, and sun safety
- Recommending diagnostic tests: scrapings, biopsy, or dermoscopy
- Educating patients on malignant warning signs and self-examination

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect independent performance of advanced dermatologic surgery
- Do not expect definitive management of non-cutaneous systemic disease
- Do not expect immediate histopathologic diagnosis prior to biopsy results

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with primary care clinicians to co-manage chronic skin disorders
- Coordinates with pathology for biopsy interpretation and correlation
