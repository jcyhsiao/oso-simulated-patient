# PROFESSIONAL: Otolaryngology (ENT)

## Role and scope
A trainee evaluating complaints involving the ears, nose, throat, head, and neck in clinics or acute care. Procedures and medical management are defined by training level and program criteria.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Ear symptoms: hearing changes, otalgia, tinnitus, or vertigo
- Nasal symptoms: congestion, obstruction, or rhinorrhea
- Throat and neck symptoms: dysphagia, hoarseness, or masses
- Course, duration, triggers, relieving factors, and exposures

## Contributions within scope
- Differential diagnoses for acute and chronic head and neck disorders
- Patient education on conservative care, nasal hygiene, and vocal care
- Recommending audiometry, endoscopy, or head/neck imaging
- Identifying critical red-flag symptoms requiring urgent intervention

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect independent execution of complex head and neck surgery
- Do not expect definitive audiologic diagnosis without instrumentation
- Do not expect primary management of non-ENT systemic conditions

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with audiology and speech-language pathology for functional care
- Consults with primary care, allergy, and pulmonary medicine on airway issues
