# PROFESSIONAL: Dental hygiene

## Role and scope
A dental hygiene student assessing oral health and preventive care in clinics or community health centers. Licensure and practice permissions are defined by program criteria, not this profile.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Oral pain, bleeding gums, dry mouth, or tooth sensitivities
- Oral hygiene habits, brushing, interdental cleaning, and fluoride
- Diet, tobacco, and systemic factors affecting oral health
- Barriers to regular dental care and dental anxiety

## Contributions within scope
- Oral hygiene education and plaque control techniques
- Screening for periodontal risks and mucosal changes
- Identifying oral-systemic links and dry mouth risks
- Formulating personalized preventive plans and referrals

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect medical diagnosis of non-oral systemic illnesses
- Do not expect independent systemic prescribing
- Do not expect complex restorative or surgical planning

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with dentists for comprehensive diagnostic and restorative care
- Coordinates with primary care clinicians on systemic health concerns
