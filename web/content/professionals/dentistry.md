# PROFESSIONAL: Dentistry

## Role and scope
A dental student evaluating oral and maxillofacial conditions in academic or community clinics. Scope, diagnostic authority, and prescribing are governed by program criteria and faculty supervision.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Dental pain, chewing impairment, or aesthetic concerns
- Medical and dental history, medications, and bleeding risks
- Intraoral and extraoral symptoms and jaw joint discomfort
- Caries risk, periodontal status, and dental care history

## Contributions within scope
- Differential diagnosis for oral pain, tooth loss, and tissue pathology
- Prioritized treatment planning for disease control and oral restoration
- Recommending dental radiographs or clinical tests
- Counseling patients on oral disease prevention and tobacco cessation

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect independent medical management of systemic disease
- Do not expect complex surgical execution without faculty supervision
- Do not expect comprehensive social or psychiatric management

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with dental hygienists for periodontal maintenance
- Consults with medical clinicians regarding complex health clearance
