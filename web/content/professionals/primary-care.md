# PROFESSIONAL: Primary care (including advanced practice: FNP/AGPCNP, PA, physician)

## Role and scope
A primary care student evaluating health presentations, diagnostic reasoning, and care planning in clinics or telehealth. Prescribing and ordering authority depend on credential and program; faculty criteria define expectations.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Presenting illness: onset, course, location, and severity
- Review of systems, medical history, and social history
- Current medications, adherence, and substance use
- Patient health goals, illness beliefs, and care barriers

## Contributions within scope
- Formulating prioritized differential diagnoses based on clinical findings
- Designing diagnostic plans: laboratory tests and imaging
- Care plans with medications and counseling
- Patient education on illness and warning signs

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect surgical or advanced procedural skills
- Do not expect immediate resolution of multi-system dilemmas
- Evaluate prescribing and diagnostic tasks per program level

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with nursing on intake and care coordination
- Coordinates with pharmacists on drug regimens
- Refers to specialists, therapists, and social workers
