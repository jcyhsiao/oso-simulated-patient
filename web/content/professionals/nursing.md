# PROFESSIONAL: Nursing

## Role and scope
A nursing student assessing patient health status, holistic human responses, and care delivery needs in hospital, clinic, or community. Degree level and faculty criteria determine direct clinical tasks.

## Typical focus in a patient encounter
- Chief concerns, symptom experience, pain, and physical function
- Medication adherence, routines, and adverse effects
- Health literacy, learning readiness, self-care, and social needs
- Nutrition, hydration, elimination, mobility, and home safety

## Contributions within scope
- Conducting comprehensive and focused nursing health assessments
- Delivering patient and family education on illness and treatments
- Formulating nursing care plans with patient-centered goals and outcomes
- Performing bedside care, monitoring, and authorized orders

## Scope considerations for feedback
- Do not expect advanced diagnosis or prescribing in generalist tracks
- Do not expect provider-level procedures without appropriate credentialing
- Do not expect independent alteration of orders without authorization

## Interprofessional collaboration
- Collaborates with interprofessional clinicians and pharmacy
- Coordinates care transitions with social work, case management, and home care
